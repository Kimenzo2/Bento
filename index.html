How Genesis Works

Getting started takes less than two minutes. First, you choose one of the three creative realms — Cosmos, Kingdom, or Cell — which sets the tone, vocabulary, and visual palette that Gen will work within. This context primes the AI before you type a single word, so the output is always coherent and thematically consistent.

Next, you describe your idea in plain language. No prompt engineering, no special syntax. Tell Gen what you want to create — a story, an educational guide, a world map, a character sheet — and it asks clarifying questions to sharpen the vision. You stay in control; Genesis does the heavy lifting.

Finally, you refine your creation in the Visual Studio. Adjust styles, swap scenes, edit text, and regenerate individual panels until everything is exactly right. When you're satisfied, export as a polished PDF or eBook, or share a live link that anyone can view on any device — no account required to read.
